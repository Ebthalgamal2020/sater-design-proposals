Choose a style, measure the inside of your cot, and we prepare your request as one message for you to send us.

How to measure

  • Measure inside the cot rails, where the bumper will sit.
  • Write the long side as length and the short side as width, in centimetres.
  • Tell us about anything unusual, such as a drop side or rounded corners.
