An editorial shop shaped by a review of four Egyptian baby brands. Images sit in soft “nest rings”, a baby nest seen from above.

Competitor review · for V6

What we took, and what we left out

Reviewed: My Little World, The Maree Shop, Yoya and Bunny, Luly Baby. Ideas only; no code, copy or designs copied.

Used in V6

  • Founder-led story and a dedicated custom-order route (My Little World)
  • Category shortcuts and a clear Shop menu (Luly Baby, Maree)
  • Variant choices on product cards, plus filter chips (Yoya and Bunny)
  • Delivery and returns stated plainly, with order help always within reach (Luly Baby)

Waiting on SATER

  • Customer reviews and order counts: only real reviews can be shown
  • Bundles and sets: only if SATER offers them
  • Prices, free-shipping thresholds, cash on delivery, payment icons
  • Arabic/English toggle: every competitor has one; needs approved Arabic copy
  • “Bestseller” and “New” badges: need real sales data

Business decisions

  • Ordering: through WhatsApp/Instagram, or online checkout (and which payment methods)
  • Languages: Arabic only, or Arabic and English
  • Product variants, sizes, fabrics, colours and prices
  • Custom order steps, measuring guide and lead times
  • Delivery areas, fees and timing
  • WhatsApp number, phone, email
  • English name and meaning of the “بورط” category
  • Whether founders appear by name or photo

Assets and setup

  • Original product and lifestyle photos (list above)
  • Official transparent logo files (SVG or PNG) for light backgrounds
  • Platform, hosting and domain, decided once ordering is settled
  • Approval of new draft lines, for example V3’s “Made by mothers. Tested on our own babies.”

All approved copy (Our Story, Exchange & Return Policy) is used word for word in every version.
