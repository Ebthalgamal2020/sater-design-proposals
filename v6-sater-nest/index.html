Our story · founded by mothers

Our Story 🤍

Sater started from a real experience… not just an idea.

We are mothers first.
We’ve been through the same journey — the worries, the small details, and the constant need to keep our babies safe and comfortable.

We tried so many products…
Some looked nice, but didn’t really work.
Some made things harder instead of easier.

So we decided to create what we wished we had.

Every product at Sater is tested by us, on our own babies.
Designed to solve real problems we faced as moms.

Our goal is simple:
To make motherhood a little easier…
And a lot more comfortable for both mom and baby 👶🏻✨

Exchange & Return Policy

At Sater, your comfort and satisfaction are our priority 🤍

We offer exchange and return on all products under the following conditions:

  • Items must be in their original condition, unused, and in the same quality as received.
  • Requests must be made within 7 days from the delivery date.
  • Customers are responsible for return shipping fees.
  • Refunds are processed via InstaPay only.

Customized Products

Please note that all customized or made-to-measure items (including bed bumpers or any products made in specific sizes) are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

However, exceptions apply only in the following cases:

  • If the product has a manufacturing defect.
  • If there is a mistake in the size.
  • If there is any fault in the product itself.

In these cases only, we will offer an exchange or return for the customized item.
